The Proprietor
Arjun Patel

Crafting spaces
with intention

Born and raised in Nairobi, I grew up surrounded by the vivid colours, textures and stories woven into East African life. After completing a degree in Interior Architecture at the University of Nairobi and a postgraduate diploma in spatial design in London, I returned home with one clear purpose — to build spaces that honour where we come from while embracing where we're headed.

"Every room has a personality waiting to be discovered. My job is simply to listen."

Over twelve years, I've had the privilege of designing for families in Karen, corporate suites in Westlands, boutique hotels on the Coast and restaurants in the CBD. Each project is treated as a singular conversation — never a formula. At Nayls Enterprise, we don't do templates. We do you.

How We Work

Our design process

01
Discovery Call

We start with a conversation — your vision, lifestyle, timeline and budget. This shapes everything that follows.

02
